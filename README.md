**B. Synopsis**
  _**- 50 First Dates (2004) is a romantic comedy following Henry Roth, a playboy Hawaiian veterinarian who avoids commitment until he falls for Lucy Whitmore. After a charming initial spark, Henry discovers that Lucy suffers from short-term memory loss due to a past car accident, resetting her memory every night when she sleeps. Determined to be with her, Henry dedicates himself to making Lucy fall in love with him all over again every single day. He creates daily video tapes to gently update her on her life and their evolving relationship so she can wake up without confusion. Ultimately, the film presents a heartwarming story about steadfast dedication and finding creative ways to build a life together despite extraordinary obstacles.**_

**C. Characters**
- Henry Roth –> A charming marine veterinarian living in Hawaii who avoids long-term commitments until he meets Lucy. He shows incredible devotion by inventing creative, daily ways to win her heart and help her navigate her condition.
- Lucy Whitmore –> An artistic, bright high school art teacher who suffers from anterograde amnesia after a car accident. Because her memory wipes every night, she wakes up every morning believing it is the day before her accident.
- Marlin Whitmore –> Lucy’s protective father, a veteran fisherman who works tirelessly with Lucy's brother to recreate the exact day of her accident every day to spare her from the painful realization of her condition.
- Doug Whitmore –> Lucy’s eccentric, lisping brother who is obsessed with bodybuilding and fitness. Along with their father, he goes to extreme lengths daily to preserve Lucy's routine and protect her emotional well-being.
- Ula –> Henry’s fun-loving, loyal Hawaiian best friend who offers humor, quirky advice, and moral support while helping Henry carry out his daily schemes to woo Lucy.
